Finish qualification of the frozen bulk-fragment/realm candidate before measuring another source revision. Selection: retain the opaque arena and weak private NodeList data. Nine-process pure query-all publication improves 5.651→3.887 ms at 256 rows and 4.817→4.030 ms at 8,192 rows per 8,192 calls. Sampled five-batch publication allocation falls 43.16→12.24 MiB. Complete result iteration remains flat; diverse cache-missing queries do not benefit. The base-Parser fragment variant is approximately flat for tables; it does not establish recovery of the earlier small-table regression.
